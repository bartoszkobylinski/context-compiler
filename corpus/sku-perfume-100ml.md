# Fjord Mist Perfume 100 ml — Shipping Specification

Fjord Mist perfume is classified by Northstar Fulfillment as a restricted flammable cosmetic for transport planning.

A 100 ml retail bottle may use an approved air service only when packed in Restricted Goods Pack H2 and accepted by the selected carrier's restricted-goods rules.

Do not infer carrier eligibility from the product specification alone; follow the carrier restricted-goods rules.

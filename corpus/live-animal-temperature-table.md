# Live Animal Temperature Limits
Ornamental gecko: acceptable handling temperature 12–28°C; shipment must not be accepted when forecast ramp temperature is below 10°C or above 30°C. Small tropical fish: 15–26°C. Domestic rabbit: 5–24°C. These limits apply at origin, transfer points, and destination handoff.

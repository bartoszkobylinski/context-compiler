# Northern Route Road Closure Advisory
E8 freight traffic north of Nordkjosbotn is subject to intermittent closure from 20:00 to 02:00. NordGround services to Tromsø must add a 10-hour contingency delay for departures handed over after 16:00 on 12 September 2026.

# Remote Address Access Policy
Remote residential addresses flagged LIMITED ACCESS cannot use unattended leave-at-door delivery for high-value goods. Signature handoff is mandatory. Delivery promises are subject to the last-mile access calendar and cannot be inferred from trunk-service arrival alone.

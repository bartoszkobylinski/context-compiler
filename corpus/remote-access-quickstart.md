# Remote Access Quickstart

## Personal laptops and VPN

Contractors working remotely may use a personal laptop to connect through the company VPN when remote access has been granted.

VPN is mandatory for remote connections. Device encryption should be enabled before connecting.

This quickstart is operational guidance only. Access to restricted or customer data remains subject to the current Security Policy.

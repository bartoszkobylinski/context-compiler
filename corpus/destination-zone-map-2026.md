# Destination Zone Map 2026
Tromsø is zone N3. Svalbard is zone N5. Bergen is zone W1. N3 supports PolarExpress Air, NordGround Battery, NordGround Secure, and ColdRoute Active. Live-animal services are available only when the destination animal desk is open and no operational bulletin suspends acceptance.

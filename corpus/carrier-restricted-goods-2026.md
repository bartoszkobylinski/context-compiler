# Carrier Restricted Goods Manual

## Mixed restricted goods

A parcel containing a standalone lithium battery product may not also contain a restricted flammable cosmetic.

## PolarExpress Air Next-Day

Restricted flammable cosmetics may use PolarExpress Air Next-Day when the product specification permits air transport and Restricted Goods Pack H2 is used.

Standalone lithium battery products are not accepted on PolarExpress Air Next-Day.

For standalone lithium battery products, use an approved battery service listed in the Service Catalog and Battery Pack B1.

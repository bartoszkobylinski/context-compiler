# Weather Disruption Policy
Operational weather bulletins override normal service promises when they impose a safety restriction. A service may remain commercially available while a specific cargo class is temporarily suspended. The planner must evaluate the restriction for the shipment class, cutoff time, and destination rather than treating a service-open status as approval for every shipment.

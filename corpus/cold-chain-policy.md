# Cold Chain Handling Policy
Shipments marked 2–8°C must remain inside that range from pack-out through carrier handoff. Standard insulated packaging is not sufficient unless the product-specific hold-time requirement is met. If projected transit plus disruption buffer exceeds validated hold time, the shipment must be held or moved to an approved active-temperature service.

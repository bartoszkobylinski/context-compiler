# Live Animal Packaging SOP
L1 ventilated crate is required for reptiles and rabbits. It may not share an outer parcel with dry ice, flammable goods, lithium batteries, or glass liquids. Heat packs are permitted only when the species sheet and weather bulletin together require them.

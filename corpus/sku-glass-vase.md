# Hand-Blown Glass Vase Product Specification
The vase is a fragile glass item, 42 cm high, 2.8 kg, declared retail value NOK 8,900. It requires G4 glass-protection packaging and upright handling. The product is not restricted goods and may use standard parcel networks if insurance and signature requirements are satisfied.

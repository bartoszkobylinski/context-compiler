# ArcticBio Research Kit Product Specification
SKU BIO-28 must remain between 2°C and 8°C. Maximum validated excursion outside that range is 30 minutes during controlled handoff only. The product may use passive C24 packaging or an approved active-temperature service; standard express packaging is not permitted.

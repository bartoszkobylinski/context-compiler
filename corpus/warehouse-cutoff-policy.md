# Warehouse Dispatch Cutoff Policy
Same-day handoff to PolarExpress requires release by 15:30 local time. Restricted goods require release by 14:45 because a specialist inspection is mandatory. Live animals require acceptance by 14:00 unless an operational bulletin imposes an earlier cutoff.

# Fragile Glass Shipping Policy
Glass containers must use an approved glass-protection workflow. Glass containing flammable liquid is governed by both fragile-glass and restricted-goods rules; satisfying one does not waive the other. Items above NOK 5,000 declared value also require the high-value release rule.

# PB20 Power Bank 20,000 mAh — Shipping Specification

The PB20 is treated by Northstar Fulfillment as a standalone lithium battery product for carrier selection.

The PB20 must use an approved battery service and Battery Pack B1.

The PB20 may not be combined in the same parcel with a restricted flammable cosmetic under Northstar Fulfillment rules.

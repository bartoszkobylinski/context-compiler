# Live Animal Service Catalog
PolarExpress Animal Priority serves Tromsø Monday–Friday with destination animal-desk handoff before 16:00. PolarExpress Air Next-Day is not live-animal approved. No live-animal service is released when an operational weather bulletin suspends animal acceptance for the relevant handoff window.

# Remote Area Last-Mile Calendar
For the Senja LIMITED ACCESS zone, staffed signature delivery operates Monday, Wednesday, and Friday between 12:00 and 18:00. There is no staffed delivery on Sunday 13 September 2026. Parcels arriving at the regional depot on Sunday are held until the next staffed window.

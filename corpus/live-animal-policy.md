# Live Animal Transport Policy
Live animals may ship only on services explicitly marked LIVE-ANIMAL APPROVED. Shipment must satisfy species temperature limits, packaging requirements, acceptance cutoff, and no-weekend-hold rule. A commercially open route is not enough to approve an animal shipment.

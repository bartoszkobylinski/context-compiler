# Glass Packaging SOP
G4 glass-protection kit is required for retail glass containers up to 1 litre. Each bottle must be individually sleeved and separated by rigid dividers. G4 cannot replace H2 when the glass container holds a restricted flammable cosmetic; both workflows must be satisfied in a compatible outer pack.

# Carrier Insurance Thresholds 2026
PolarExpress Air Next-Day offers declared-value cover up to NOK 10,000. NordGround Secure offers cover up to NOK 50,000 and signature on delivery. Standard Economy offers only NOK 2,500 cover. Coverage limits are per parcel, not per order.

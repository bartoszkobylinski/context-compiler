# Cold Chain Packaging SOP
Pack C8 is validated for 2–8°C shipments for 36 hours at ambient temperatures from -5°C to 20°C. Pack C24 is validated for 72 hours under the same conditions. Neither validation applies if a shipment contains live animals, flammable liquids, or loose lithium batteries. Pack availability must be confirmed before release.

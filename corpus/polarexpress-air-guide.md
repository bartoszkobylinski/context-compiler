# PolarExpress Air Next-Day Quick Guide

PolarExpress Air Next-Day serves mainland Norway, including Tromsø.

Most retail cosmetics and consumer electronics are eligible for Air Next-Day when packed under their product-specific rules.

Restricted goods, batteries, and mixed restricted-goods parcels are subject to the Carrier Restricted Goods Manual. The quick guide is not sufficient to approve a restricted-goods shipment.

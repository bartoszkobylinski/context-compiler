# Restricted Goods Packaging SOP

Restricted Goods Pack H2 is the approved pack for eligible retail flammable cosmetics.

Battery Pack B1 is the approved pack for standalone lithium battery products using an approved battery service.

H2 and B1 are separate packaging workflows and must not be combined into one parcel.

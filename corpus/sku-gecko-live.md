# Ornamental Gecko Transport Specification
The gecko must use L1 ventilated live-animal packaging. Handling temperature must remain within the ornamental-gecko limits. The animal must not be routed into an overnight network hold, combined with restricted goods, or tendered after a weather bulletin suspends live-animal acceptance.

# Fulfillment Service Catalog

PolarExpress Air Next-Day is available for eligible air shipments to Tromsø.

NordGround Battery is the approved battery service for standalone lithium battery products to Tromsø.

NordGround Battery is not a next-business-day service; its standard promise is two to three business days.

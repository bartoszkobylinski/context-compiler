# Temperature Controlled Service Catalog
ColdRoute Active is approved for 2–8°C shipments to Tromsø with continuous active control and a standard 48-hour promise. PolarExpress Air Next-Day is not a validated active-temperature service. NordGround Standard has no temperature-control certification.

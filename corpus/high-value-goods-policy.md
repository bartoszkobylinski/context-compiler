# High Value Goods Release Policy
Goods with declared value above NOK 5,000 require signature service and an insurance option covering at least the declared value. Goods above NOK 20,000 additionally require supervisor release. A fast service that lacks sufficient insurance cannot be selected solely to preserve delivery speed.
